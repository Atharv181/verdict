How a verdict moves money

1 · LockPayer locks USDC against a rubric. Only keccak(rubric) is stored.
2 · EvidencePayee commits keccak(evidence). The prompt will name both hashes.
3 · JudgeRelayer calls 4 TeeML models directly, keeps the exact request and response bytes.
4 · SignEach enclave signs sha256(req):sha256(resp) with its on-chain key.
5 · SettleContract re-hashes, ecrecovers, checks the registry and the header, moves USDC.
